2. Mathematical Model & Boundary Calculation

At 09:20:00 IST, when the first 5-minute candle closes, the reference price X is captured:

Reference Close (X) = Close of 09:15–09:20 Candle
Upper Breakout Level (+0.09%) = X × (1 + 0.09 / 100) = X × 1.0009
Lower Breakout Level (−0.09%) = X × (1 − 0.09 / 100) = X × 0.9991
Locked ATM Strike = round(X / 50) × 50
Level Invariance Rule: Once calculated at 09:20:00 IST, the Upper Level, Lower Level, and Locked ATM Strike remain 100% fixed for the remainder of the trading day. They are never updated or shifted intrabar.

3. ATM Option Strike & Contract Resolution

At 09:20:00 IST, the engine connects to the live Dhan Option Chain API and resolves the exact weekly option contracts corresponding to the locked strike:

  • ATM CE: e.g., NIFTY [Current Weekly Expiry] [Locked Strike] CE
  • ATM PE: e.g., NIFTY [Current Weekly Expiry] [Locked Strike] PE

Both contracts are monitored concurrently. Orders are dispatched directly to the exchange NFO segment using their official Dhan Security IDs.
