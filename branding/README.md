# Mavrix identity

The new identity uses a geometric white **M** and muted blue **V** on solid navy. The built-in ImageGen tool generated the concept; the production artwork was redrawn as flat SVG geometry for crisp edges and uniform colors. There are no gradients or lighting effects in the production assets.

## Files

- `generated-concept.png`: the ImageGen design reference.
- `../public/brand/mavrix-mark.svg`: scalable production app mark.
- `../public/brand/mavrix-logo.svg`: horizontal Mavrix Trading lockup.
- `../public/brand/icon-1024.png`: high-resolution icon master.
- `../public/brand/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`: PWA icons.
- `../public/brand/icon-maskable-512.png`: full-bleed icon with safe monogram margins.
- `../public/brand/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180px iPhone Home Screen icon.
- `../public/brand/icon-16.png`, `icon-32.png`, `icon-48.png`, `favicon.ico`: browser icons.

The original `/mavrix-icon.jpg` and `/favicon.svg` URLs also contain the new identity for compatibility. All current UI references use the new `/brand/` URLs. No application dependencies or authentication settings were changed.

## Palette

Navy `#102035`, white `#FFFFFF`, muted blue `#597BBA`.

## ImageGen prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished app icon for MAVRIX, a professional trading platform. A distinctive bold geometric M monogram, custom architectural letterform using two clean ascending strokes and a crisp central V of negative space. Confident, simple, attractive, highly legible at 24px. Flat white monogram with one restrained muted blue #597BBA accent, on a completely solid deep navy #102035 full-bleed square background. The monogram is centered and occupies approximately 58% of the canvas width and 48% height, with generous maskable-icon safe margins. Exact orthographic flat vector-like appearance with exceptionally clean edges and consistent visual weight. Icon only, no words, no tiny detail, no charts, no candlesticks, no currency symbols, no decorative frame. No gradients, neon, glow, shadows, lighting effects, textures, transparency, 3D or mockups. Square production-ready logo master.

## Refinement prompt

Refine this exact Mavrix icon, preserving the white M and the small blue V geometry, size, and centered placement. Make it a truly flat 2D logo: EVERY background pixel must be uniform solid #102035, white areas uniform #FFFFFF, blue areas uniform #597BBA, allowing only normal edge anti-aliasing. Remove ALL texture, lighting, shading, grain, gradients, shadow, vignette, metallic or embossed effects. No words or extra marks. Full bleed square, not a presentation or mockup. Production app icon with crisp flat shapes.
